Minimal Monogram

Concept 1: The Apex Chevron

Sharp, interlocking "V" monogram (Victory / Invictus). Dynamic faceted geometry with Electric Volt and Cream wings, forming an upward triumphal compass diamond. Extreme legibility at all sizes.

Modernist Silhouette

Concept 2: The Spartan/Gladiator Helm

Bauhaus-level geometric reduction of the unconquered gladiator crest. Zero clutter, zero tiny wires or cyber eyes. Bold monolithic silhouette with an iconic T-visor slit on electric volt.

Architectural Monogram

Concept 3: The Invictus Monolith ("I")

Architectural Roman "I" pillar fused with an upward spear chevron. Represents unwavering strength and discipline ("I am the master of my fate"). Modern Swiss typographic restraint.

Omni Emblem

Concept 4: The 4-Pillars Omni Shield

Faceted diamond shield embodying the 4 core pillars of the OmniTracker: Finance (Volt), Habits (Cream), Study (Titanium), and Tasks (Emerald). Fintech-grade minimalism (Stripe / Linear caliber).
