## 2. Neobrutalist Visual Identity & Animation Standards
- **Color Tokens**: `#FAF8F5` cream surfaces, deep `#161514` obsidian ink borders, and vibrant badges (`#CEF431` electric lime, `#03D26F` malachite emerald, `#C084FC` lavender).
- **Hard Ink Shadows**: Zero-blur hard drop shadows (`shadow-[2px_2px_0px_0px_#161514]` and `shadow-[3px_3px_0px_0px_#161514]`).
- **Typography**: Heavy geometric display typography (`font-heading font-black`) paired with crisp monospace data telemetry (`font-mono`).
- **Emil Kowalski Motion Rules**:
  - Animate only `transform` and `opacity`.
  - Never scale from 0 (scale from `0.95` to `1`).
  - Contextual duration: 150ms–300ms with spring or ease-out curves.
  - Hardware acceleration (`will-change: transform`) and reduced-motion support.
